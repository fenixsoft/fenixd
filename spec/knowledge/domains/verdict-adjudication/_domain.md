# 验证裁决与证据

验证裁决与证据。本域能力条目清单如下（能力页由存量回填批次逐步产出，落盘后此处升级为链接）。

## 能力页

- `verifier-judgment-integrity` — verifier 判定完整性
- `verdict-contract-singularity` — 裁决契约单一性
- `verdict-result-integrity` — 裁决结果完整性（fail-closed 读取）
- `verdict-failure-semantics` — 裁决失败语义
- `verdict-failure-passthrough` — 失败结论透传
- `verdict-evidence-taxonomy` — 裁决证据分类
- `verdict-attribution-evidence-gate` — 裁决归因证据门
- `attribution-freshness-governance` — 归因新鲜度治理
- `screenshot-evidence-labeling` — 截图证据标注
- `verifier-selector-evidence` — verifier 选择器证据
- `verified-gate-evidence` — verified 门证据持久化
- `verdict-dedup-resynthesis` — 裁决去重与重合成
- `verdict-cache-reverify` — 裁决缓存复验
- `verdict-visibility` — 裁决可见性（Dashboard 呈现）
- `judge-artifact-gate` — judge 产物硬门
- `judge-artifact-checkpoint` — judge 产物检查点
- `judge-incremental-attribution` — judge 增量归因
- `judge-rerun-governance` — judge 重跑治理
- `inconclusive-loop-escalation` — 无结论循环升级
